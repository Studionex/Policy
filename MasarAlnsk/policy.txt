This Privacy Policy explains how Masar Alnisk ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects personal and sensitive user data when using our mobile application and services ("the App").

Our system is an intelligent platform designed for managing campaigns, groups, and field personnel, using organizational hierarchies, interactive maps, reporting tools, messaging systems, and smart analytics to ensure efficient operations and safety.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data:

a) Personal Information

Full name

Phone number

National Id (not required)

Email address

User role (Owner, Admin, Supervisor, Worker, Driver)

Organization or campaign affiliation

b) Location Data

Real-time GPS location (only when the app is actively in use)

Movement and positioning data for operational tracking and safety purposes

c) Usage Data

Login activity

Feature usage

Reports and messages submitted

System interactions for analytics and performance improvement

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data for the following purposes:

Managing campaigns, groups, and workforce operations

Real-time location tracking to ensure safety and coordination

Displaying personnel locations on interactive maps

Handling emergency and operational incident reports

Enabling direct communication between management and field teams

Generating analytical insights for operational efficiency

Improving system performance, reliability, and security

Complying with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Location Data Usage

We collect location data only when required for system functionality, including:

Real-time tracking of workers during campaigns

Emergency response and safety monitoring

Operational planning and logistics management

Location data is not used for advertising purposes and is never sold to third parties.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

We may share limited data only with:

Authorized system administrators within your organization

Trusted cloud service providers for hosting and security

Legal authorities if required by law

All data sharing follows strict confidentiality and security standards.

5. Data Storage and Security

We apply industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including:

Encrypted communication (SSL / HTTPS)

Secure cloud storage

Role-based access control

Continuous system monitoring

We take all reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Data Retention

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to:

Operate the system

Comply with legal requirements

Maintain security and operational integrity

You may request deletion of your data through your organization’s system administrator.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

Access your personal data

Request correction of incorrect data

Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)

Requests can be submitted via your organization’s system administrator or by contacting us directly.
